FAQ

Quick answers for membership and onboarding.

Is SRC membership paid?

No, the membership flow is designed as a student-friendly, accessible entry point.

How long does the application take?

The form should take less than three minutes if the applicant already has their details ready.

Can final-year students apply?

Yes, if they are still enrolled and genuinely interested in research, mentorship, or publication support.

What happens after submission?

The SRC team reviews applications in batches and responds with next steps, usually within a few working days.
